# PhotoKeep

PhotoKeep is a photo digitization service. We pick up your old printed photos, scan them at high resolution, and deliver them back to you as digital files — organized, backed up, and ready to share.

## Services

- **Standard scan** — clean digital copies of your photos
- **Enhanced scan** — colour correction and basic restoration included
- **Premium scan** — full restoration, scratch removal, and enhancement

USB drive delivery available as an add-on.
